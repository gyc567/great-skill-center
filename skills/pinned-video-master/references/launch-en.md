# Getting Started (English)

## What this does
Turns your professional background into a **30-second pinned video** that tells a
stranger who you are, what you've done, and how you can help them.

Built for personal-brand founders, knowledge creators, and consultants — one video
replaces ten introductions.

## How it works (3 steps)

### Step 1: Build your dossier (required, ~3-5 min)
First run creates `pinned-dossier.md` with six modules: identity, history, current work,
offering, proof, and presence (platform/style).

I'll ask **one question at a time**. Anything you skip gets marked "TBD" — never filled
in with generic industry filler. The file lives in your own working directory.

### Step 2: Write the script
Five sections across 30 seconds:

```
Hook 0-3s → Identity 3-8s → Proof 8-20s → Now 20-27s → Value + CTA 27-30s
```

Max 90 Chinese characters / 75 English words. You get a Chinese and an English version
(English is rewritten for English rhythm, not translated word-for-word).

### Step 3: Render (optional)
After you approve the script, generate a digital-avatar video via HeyGen (uses your key).
No HeyGen? The script + storyboard still work in CapCut / other editors.

## FAQ

**Do I need the dossier?**
Recommended. Without it I can produce a one-off script labelled "no dossier, low
confidence", but it won't carry over to future sessions.

**Will the avatar look fake?**
Possibly — that's why there's a QA stage: credibility checklist + human-feel checklist
+ iteration actions, capped at 2 rounds. If it's too synthetic, cut the script in an editor
with your real footage.

**Can you guarantee views?**
No. This skill makes the content credible, specific, and human. It doesn't promise reach.

**My background isn't impressive.**
That's fine. The hook doesn't need to be world-changing — concrete detail beats polish.
